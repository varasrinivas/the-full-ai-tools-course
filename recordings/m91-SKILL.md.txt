# Threshold audit

When auditing a threshold comparison in priorauth-api:

1. Read the comparison line in `ApprovalService.process()`.
2. Compare it against the documented rule in the class javadoc and the approval log line.
3. Report in exactly this format, which is the house standard:

   THRESHOLD AUDIT
   - operator in code: <the operator>
   - documented rule: <inclusive or exclusive>
   - verdict: MATCH or MISMATCH
   - rows affected at the boundary in seed.sql: <count>

Always end with the literal line: "Audit complete (threshold-audit skill)."
