M-8.1 WHAT YOU PAY FOR — anatomy of a turn, measured
=====================================================
Recorded: 2026-07-24 · priorauth-api lab/start clone (read-only questions only;
clone untouched, no branch needed). Instrument: `--output-format json` usage
envelope (established in M-7.3), plus the per-model `modelUsage` breakdown.

=== THREE TASKS OF INCREASING AGENTIC DEPTH, SAME REPO ===
  task                    turns  cache_rd  cache_wr  new_in   out     cost
  trivial (no tools)          1    25,512         0       2     4   $0.0134
  single-file question        2    48,042     3,431       4   234   $0.0648
  multi-step exploration     10   109,867    14,484       7 3,320   $0.2834

=== THE HEADLINE: YOU DO NOT PAY FOR YOUR PROMPT ===
  On the 10-turn run the user's typed prompt was **7 tokens**. The input side of
  the bill was **124,358 tokens**. Ratio typed:carried = **1 : 17,765**.
  You are billed for the conversation the agent has with ITSELF — every tool
  result, every file it read, re-sent as context on every subsequent turn.

=== COST SCALES WITH TURNS, NOT WITH QUESTION LENGTH ===
  1 turn  $0.0134
  2 turns $0.0648   (~4.8x)
  10 turns $0.2834  (**21.1x** the trivial run; ~$0.0283 per turn)
  The prompts were 2, 4 and 7 tokens respectively — i.e. the thing the user
  controls most directly (how much they type) is the thing that matters least.
  What actually moved the bill: how many times the agent went around the loop.
  This is why M-5.1's "context quality decides your lap count" is a COST lesson
  as much as a debugging one, and why M-4.4's misrung analysis matters.

=== PER-MODEL COST ATTRIBUTION (the modelUsage field) ===
  ONE invocation, TWO models:
    claude-haiku-4-5      541 in ·    15 out ·        0 cached · $0.000616
    claude-opus-4-8[1m]     7 in · 3,320 out · 109,867 cached · $0.282809
  Haiku = **0.22%** of the bill; Opus = **99.78%**.
  So the envelope already gives per-model attribution for free, and it shows a
  small fast model doing auxiliary work alongside the main one. This is the
  data you need before any "use a cheaper model" conversation (M-8.4): the
  cheap model was never the expense.

=== READING /cost AND THE ENVELOPE ===
  Interactive: /cost reports session spend. Scripted: --output-format json gives
  total_cost_usd, num_turns, duration_ms, and usage{cache_read_input_tokens,
  cache_creation_input_tokens, input_tokens, output_tokens} plus modelUsage.
  M-7.3's caveat still governs every reading: a run that CHANGES the cached
  prefix pays cache_creation (writes cost more than reads), so compare
  steady-state to steady-state, never first-run-after-an-edit to warm-cache.

TEACHING BEATS: (1) the bill is context re-sent per turn, not your typing;
(2) turns are the cost multiplier — 21x from 1 to 10; (3) output is small in
volume but expensive per token, input is huge in volume but mostly cached, so
neither intuition alone predicts the bill — read the envelope; (4) per-model
attribution is already in the JSON, and it usually shows the expensive work is
the main model doing the loop, not the helper.
