WHY THIS IS THE MODULE
  Mob Elaboration is NOT "the AI plans the sprint." It is: the agent reads the real system,
  RESHAPES a vague business intent into what's actually true, asks the questions a senior would
  ask, decomposes into grounded, sized units of work with the reason each matters, and marks
  where a HUMAN must decide — culminating in the honest finding that "cut the queue 20%" is a
  risk-trade-off decision, not an engineering task. The value of the ceremony is that it surfaces
  that BEFORE construction, when changing course is free. Units map onto the course's own planted
  issues (BUG-API-01/03, the no-resolve-endpoint gap) because they ARE real levers on the queue.
  Ties to T-11: you literally cannot claim -20% without instrumenting first (UoW-1).
