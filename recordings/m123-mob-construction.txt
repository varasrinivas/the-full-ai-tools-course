THE STANDOUT BEAT — STOPPED at UoW-5 (the-agent-proposes-the-human-approves, made real)
  It refused to build the "claim history as a decision input" unit and wrote exactly why it
  needs humans, not a passing suite. Verbatim-trimmed:
   - "that crosses the line from a code change into a change of medical-necessity policy — so
     it needs sign-off from people who can own that, not a passing test suite."
   - a CLINICAL LEAD must approve the rule (what history means, the lookback window, whether a
     denial pattern may lower a patient's approval odds at all);
   - a COMPLIANCE/LEGAL reviewer must check payer contracts, CMS/state prior-auth rules, and
     ANTI-DISCRIMINATION exposure — "a history-based downgrade can systematically disadvantage
     patients with prior denials, which is a disparate-impact risk", plus appealable reasons;
   - the measurement first = a SHADOW DRY-RUN: compute the proposed decision alongside the
     current one for a window of live traffic, audit-log only, change nothing, then report the
     flip matrix + queue-volume delta vs the 20% target + a stratified breakdown for clinical
     hand-audit; ship only if the audit passes, behind a flag with a rollback.
   - "The yield is still unknown — the dry-run exists to produce the number, and it's legitimate
     for the answer to be that the rule doesn't earn its risk."

WHY THIS IS THE MODULE
  Mob Construction is the crew building the validated units with the human as approver, not
  typist. The run shows both halves: the agent autonomously BUILDS the safe, mechanical units
  (with tests, minimal diff, honest scope notes) AND REFUSES the unit that is a policy/risk
  decision, articulating the exact human governance it needs. That refusal is the responsible-AI
  payoff in a regulated domain and is M-1.6's decision boundary at the ceremony scale: the agent
  may build the mechanism alone; it may not decide medical-necessity policy alone. Composes the
  arc: M-12.2 elaboration -> M-12.3 construction (here) -> the diff is the PR (M-10.5). The M-10.2
  pre-commit test gate composes as the commit gate (proven in its own module; not re-wired here).
