THE LESSON
  An agent can carry a unit of work from a written intent to a reviewable PR without a human
  at the keyboard for the middle — plan, branch, build, test-to-green, commit, PR. Your leverage
  is (1) a clear issue/spec at the front, (2) the gates and policy wired in (hook, MCP, allowlist)
  so the agent can't cut a corner you care about, and (3) a diff + PR to review at the end. T-10 in
  one sentence: encode the ritual, enforce the rule, connect the world, choose where it runs — then
  let the pipeline turn an issue into a PR you sign.
